Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All health and nutrition data in our apps is stored locally on your device, and we never sell your personal information. The app does send a few anonymous product events, such as completing onboarding or starting a purchase, tied only to a random installation identifier and never to your identity or health data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the app, go to the Settings or More tab, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Food logging, meal plans, water tracking, exercise logging, and all core features work without an internet connection. Only the AI meal scanner and online food search require internet access.

How do I delete my data?

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by going to Settings > More > Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. No data is stored on our servers.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at support@mochasmindlab.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are always helpful.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund through your Apple ID account.

About Mocha's Mind Labs

We are a small software company in Vancouver, British Columbia, building health and productivity tools that respect the people using them. No subscriptions where a single purchase will do, and no business model that depends on your data.

Who is behind it

Mocha's Mind Labs Inc. was founded by Mocha Shmigelsky, a Vancouver-based developer and AI enablement consultant. She designs, builds and tests the apps herself, which is why they are opinionated and why there is a real person to email when something breaks.

The company is incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Everything we ship is built here.

How we think about your data

Health data is among the most sensitive information a person generates, so MindLab Fitness keeps it on your device. There is no account to create and no server holding your food log. That is a deliberate constraint: if we do not hold your data, we cannot lose it, and we have nothing to sell.

Where data does leave your device, such as a meal photo sent for AI analysis, we say so plainly in the privacy policy, including what is sent, who processes it and whether it is retained.
